About Rimo Global Pharma

Rimo Global Pharma is a trusted international pharmaceutical exporter and sourcing partner based in Bangladesh.

We bridge the gap between top-tier WHO-GMP and EU-GMP certified manufacturers and global healthcare seekers—serving both individual patients (B2C) and medical institutions (B2B) worldwide.

For Patients & Families (B2C Door-to-Door Delivery): If you are seeking affordable, authentic cancer or specialty treatments, we deliver life-saving medicines directly to your doorstep. A valid doctor’s prescription is required for all B2C patient orders to ensure safe and compliant care.
For Hospitals, Pharmacies & Distributors (B2B Wholesale Sourcing): We provide compliant bulk procurement, competitive export pricing, and seamless supply chain solutions for international healthcare providers and medical distributors.

Every order is handled with strict cold-chain temperature control and dispatched worldwide via trusted partners like DHL and Air Cargo to guarantee product integrity and efficacy.

How It Works

Easy ordering steps for individual patients and institutional partners.

👤 For B2C Patients (Prescription Orders)

  1. 1.
    Send Prescription: Share your valid doctor’s prescription and delivery address via WhatsApp or Email.
  2. 2.
    Verify & Confirm: Our pharmacy team confirms medicine availability, cold-chain requirements, and total cost.
  3. 3.
    Doorstep Delivery: Receive your temperature-controlled package safely at your home via DHL.

🏢 For B2B Partners (Wholesale & Institutional Orders)

  1. 1.
    Submit Inquiry: Share your required product list (INN/Generic names) and target destination.
  2. 2.
    Quotation & Compliance: Receive competitive export pricing along with COA, GMP, and export documentation details.
  3. 3.
    Global Dispatch: Fast air cargo or courier dispatch with complete cold-chain assurance.
